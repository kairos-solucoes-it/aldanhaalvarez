Paintings, drawings and travel sketchbooks

Six decades of painting the sea, the labor and the cities of the world.
Rio de Janeiro, 1948.

Fishermen facing an adverse southwest wind. Oil on canvas, 100 x 50 cm. Enter the gallery

A gallery that begins in 1963 and is still being painted.

This collection gathers twenty four works in chronological order: early oils from the bay of Rio, the sun and salt of the Araruama Lagoon, scenes of labor and social life, and the travel sketchbooks of Lisbon, France, Dubai, Spain and beyond.

Walk through it as you would walk through a physical gallery. Every work can be viewed in full, with technique and dimensions, in the Artworks page.

The gallery begins where the painter began.
